How to Use:

  • Choose File: Click the "Choose File" button to select a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file.
  • Paste Text: Click the Paste button to insert clipboard text.
  • Drag & Drop: You can still drop a file onto the text area.
  • Pause/Resume: Click on the text area to pause or resume speech.
  • Stop: Click the Stop button to cancel speech at any time.
  • Speed: Adjust the slider to change the speech rate. Reading restarts from the current position with the new speed.
